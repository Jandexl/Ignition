# IGNIS scientific review release

Start with Scientific_Lead_Decision.md. Scientific_Methodology.md gives the interpretation, limitations and controlled experiment. This release supersedes the numerical spread-rate count of two in the earlier selected audit. The approved scope is published concurrent SIBAL/cotton summary measurements. The earlier PMMA compilation and BASS test inventory address different questions.

## File and counting rules

- ignis_table1_rates.csv contains the eight paper rows and is the canonical detailed measurement table. ignis_published_spread_rates.csv exposes the **same eight** in the earlier common outcome schema (m/s = cm/s divided by 100). Use one view per analysis. Each record_id is a source-scoped identity. Holder and specimen dimensions are distinct. NA records the source’s n/a. TC positions are distances from the upstream end separated by a vertical bar. height_estimated applies solely to flame height. The manuscript reports Saffire I–IV atmospheres at hatch closure. Flight V was depressurized and enriched.
- ignis_psi99_rate_claims.csv preserves the two original archive values (mm/s), archival run IDs and the corresponding canonical sample. Their header refers to length despite rate units. Flow is 20 for both archive rows, whereas Olson gives 25 for II-6. PSI-100 gives 30 for III. The source of each value remains explicit. Identical rates do not independently validate measurement accuracy.
- ignis_spread_rates_reviewed.csv retains all 23 audited Urban Fig. 10 markers, with comparison to the new table in its final five columns. Use spread_cm_s_redigitized for the audited reading. The differences are figure minus table. Five probable Saffire links use series, direction, flight/flow and rate. Four of these markers represent I, III and the two II samples. The IV link additionally depends on a disputed sample number. The figure’s approximately ±0.01 cm/s reading envelope measures digitization precision only. BASS 1.2 cm at 19 cm/s differs by +0.044 cm/s from its candidate marker; BASS 2.2 cm at 22 differs by +0.019 cm/s. Both exceed that reading envelope. Keep them unresolved. The other 16 markers include opposed flow and other BASS settings. No V cotton marker appears in the SIBAL figure. Never sum 8 + 23 + 2 or infer a pooled independent-run count.
- ignis_urban_table2_flame_sizes.csv keeps the earlier six source rows as published; the empty rate column is intentional. ignis_comparison_cases.csv contains derived descriptive results. ignis_comparability_matrix.csv retains the legacy series audit and appends run-level comparisons explicitly scoped to Olson. Legacy unknowns and mismatches describe the older sources. Use the new case file for the three current decisions. ignis_source_manifest.csv identifies the primary records and prior audit scope.

## Source conflicts to retain

| Item | Source statements and handling |
|---|---|
| Saffire IV and V identities | Olson Table 1 labels IV SIBAL sample 4 and V cotton sample 5. Its Fig. 1b labels those positions ignition 1 and test 2, consistent with Urban’s IV-1 and V-2. Material, upstream position, dimensions and flow support a common burn. Use IV-SIBAL and V-COTTON for display. Confirm physical sample versus ignition IDs against the card/flight log before a hard archive join. |
| Duct height | Olson defines full L × W × H and gives H = 51 or 30.5 cm for Saffire and 7.6 cm for BASS. For centered Saffire the nominal plane-to-wall distances are 25.5 and 15.25 cm. Urban prose gives about 25 and 15, consistent with one-sided clearance, while Urban Table 2 labels 46 and 30 as duct height. The 46 also equals Olson’s duct width. Preserve all definitions. Do not swap axes or halve a number already representing clearance. A nominal BASS half-height of 3.8 cm is geometry-derived and requires the installation drawing for point-level use. |
| Holder and BASS labels | Olson V holder prints 109 × 6 cm, narrower than the 40.6 cm specimen and inconsistent with its full-width card description. Retained verbatim. Narrow BASS sample number prints as two stacked 1s, transcribed 11. GMT222-T11 is a plausible inventory candidate based on width and speed. A BASS sample number, test number and as-run number need separate verification. Sample 4 likewise lacks a proven plot-to-log mapping. |
| Atmosphere and size | Urban Table 2 gives I–III 21%/100 kPa, IV 22%/100 kPa and V 26.2%/70.7 kPa. Olson gives flight-specific values, 29 instead of 30 cm for II lengths and 40.6 instead of 41 cm for wide specimens. Keep each source’s precision and atmosphere reference. Do not assign oxygen or pressure to an unmatched Figure 10 point. |

Original camera calibration, synchronized logs, actual flow histories, uncertainties, and resolutions of alias and holder conflicts remain open. The archive comparison uses the prior IGNIS field-level PSI transcription. No original flight data were newly recovered in this review.
